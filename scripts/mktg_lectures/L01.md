# Lecture 1: Introduction to Marketing: Understanding Customers, Markets, Brands and Value

Customers, Markets, Brands and Value
Overview of the Course
This course introduces marketing as a comprehensive business discipline that extends far beyond selling
products or creating advertisements. Marketing is fundamentally about understanding customer needs,
analyzing how people make purchasing decisions, exploring brand preferences, and enabling businesses to
create meaningful value for their customers.
What Marketing Really Means
Marketing is not limited to selling or product promotion
It encompasses understanding consumer behavior and decision-making processes
It explores why customers prefer certain brands over competitors
It focuses on creating value rather than just transacting sales
Marketing in Everyday Life
Marketing permeates all aspects of daily consumer experience, including:
Mobile devices and technology we use
Clothing and fashion choices
Food and dining preferences
Mobile applications and digital platforms
Brand trust and loyalty decisions
Key Learning Objectives
By the end of this course, students will understand:
How companies identify and analyze customer needs
Product and service design strategies
Pricing strategies and value determination
Brand building and development
Effective communication and marketing messaging
Methods for establishing and maintaining long-term customer relationships
Course Significance
This course aims to equip students with the ability to recognize and analyze marketing in their everyday
lives while developing a comprehensive understanding of marketing as a critical business discipline within
the BAS program in management and technology.
