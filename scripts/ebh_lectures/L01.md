# Lecture 1: Introduction to Economic and Business History Course

This introductory lecture presents a comprehensive course on economic and business history offered by Professor Manu Kanchan from the School of Management and Entrepreneurship at IIT Jodhpur.
Course Overview
- Provides a comprehensive overview of how economies, markets, industries, and businesses have evolved throughout history
- Examines the influence of economic and business development on society
- Demonstrates the central role of economics and business in modern life, shaping societal values such as efficiency, productivity, and leisure
Main Course Objectives
The course focuses on three primary areas:
Conceptual Foundation
- Defines economics and business within the context of economic and business history
- Establishes foundational understanding of key terms and concepts
Historical Evolution
- Traces how economics and business ideas have evolved over time
- Explores the origins and contexts that gave birth to various economic and business concepts
- Examines how these changes have transformed the socio-cultural context of society
Relationship Between Economics and Business
- Analyzes the complex and intricate relationship between economics and business
- Explores how economic developments have influenced business practices
- Examines instances where business innovations have influenced economic thinking
Course Audience
- Designed for both graduate and undergraduate students from any educational background
- Ideal for anyone seeking to understand the origins and complexities of modern economics and business systems
